Challenge: Adaptive Learning Intelligence System

Traditional learning experiences are often designed for everyone, but optimized for no one. Learners differ in prior knowledge, pace, learning gaps, goals, and the way they understand complex concepts.

Build an AI-powered learning system that can understand a learner’s evolving knowledge state and deliver a personalized learning experience. Your solution could combine explanations, interactive learning, assessment, feedback, knowledge tracking, adaptive content, or other approaches to help learners progress more effectively.

Pro-Tip:

Functional checks are designed to evaluate your application as a whole, not just a single feature. Solutions with thoughtfully connected workflows and meaningful functionality provide evaluators with multiple opportunities to experience your application as intended. A well-rounded application is also more resilient to isolated implementation issues, reducing the likelihood of a single broken flow impacting the overall functional evaluation.
